# PRAMAAN — Verified Work Identity Platform

**PRAMAAN** is a digital work identity and trust platform that helps workers build, verify, and carry their professional reputation across employers.

It combines **verified work identity, digital passports, QR verification, review intelligence, and trust analytics** into a single platform.

## Key Features

* **Digital Work Identity** — Professional profiles with skills, experience, and employment information.
* **PRAMAAN Passport** — A portable digital representation of a worker's professional identity.
* **QR Verification** — Short-lived dynamic QR codes for secure identity verification.
* **Review Intelligence** — Reputation analysis using verification, employment linkage, review patterns, and account signals.
* **Trust Intelligence** — Trust Score, authenticity signals, employment consistency, and risk insights.
* **Worker Discovery** — Find workers using category, location, experience, rating, and verification filters.
* **Messaging & Documents** — Communicate and manage employment-related evidence.
* **Dispute Center** — Track issues related to employment, reviews, verification, and reputation.

```text
   ↓
Add Employment Evidence
   ↓
PRAMAAN Passport
   ↓
QR Verification
   ↓
Reviews & Trust Score
   ↓
Messages / Documents / Disputes
```

### Employer

```text
   ↓

   ↓

   ↓
```

## Current Status

PRAMAAN is currently a **frontend-first product prototype**. The existing implementation demonstrates the complete product experience through frontend simulations and browser-side persistence.

Production backend services, real authentication, KYC integration, cloud storage, real-time messaging, and production-grade fraud detection are planned for future phases.

## Vision

PRAMAAN aims to create a **portable and trusted digital work identity** where a worker's verified professional reputation can move with them from one employer to another.
